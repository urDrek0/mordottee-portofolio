About Me

Mordottee is a 20-year-old freelance digital artist and an active member of the Karis community. She enjoys exploring creativity through art and connecting with others who share the same passion, constantly growing and developing her own artistic style along the way.

Her work covers a variety of styles and forms, including expressive illustrations, imaginative concept art, spontaneous sketches, and charming chibi artwork. Each piece allows her to explore different ideas, characters, and moods while adding her own personal touch to every creation.
